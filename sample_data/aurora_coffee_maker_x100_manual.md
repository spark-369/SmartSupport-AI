# Aurora Coffee Maker X100 — Product Manual

**Model:** AUR-X100 | **Version:** 2.1 | **Rated power:** 1350 W

## 1. What's in the box

- Aurora X100 brewer body with stainless drip tray
- 1.2 L removable water tank
- 18-bar portafilter with single and double baskets
- Plastic tamper, milk jug, descaling brush
- Quick-start guide and this manual

## 2. Safety

- Never immerse the brewer body in water; the base contains live electronics.
- Operate only on a grounded 220-240 V, 50/60 Hz outlet.
- Do not remove the portafilter while the pump is running — hot water can spray out.
- Descale every 3 months (or after 300 cycles) to keep the warranty valid.

## 3. First-time setup

1. Rinse the water tank and fill to the MAX line with fresh cold water.
2. Plug in the machine; the power ring glows amber for about 40 seconds while heating.
3. When the ring turns solid white, run one full tank flush: press the double-shot button
   and let all water pump through into the drip tray. Discard the water.
4. Wash the baskets, portafilter and jug with warm water before first use.

## 4. Brewing espresso

1. Insert the desired basket and add finely ground coffee: 7 g (single) or 16 g (double).
2. Tamp evenly with ~15 kg of pressure; the surface should look flat and glossy.
3. Lock the portafilter into the group head until the handle points 90° right.
4. Place a pre-warmed cup under the spouts.
5. Press the single or double button. Extraction should start within 4 seconds and
   produce 25 ml (single) or 60 ml (double) in 25-30 seconds.
6. To stop early, press the active button again.

### Extraction troubleshooting

| Symptom | Likely cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Coffee drips too slowly | Grind too fine or too much coffee | Coarsen grind, reduce dose |
| Coffee gushes, pale crema | Grind too coarse or stale beans | Finer grind, fresh beans |
| No water at all | Tank empty or machine still heating | Refill tank, wait for white ring |

## 5. Steaming milk

1. Fill the jug one-third with cold milk (max 4 °C).
2. Purge the steam wand for 2 seconds into the drip tray.
3. Submerge the wand tip 1 cm below the surface and open the steam knob.
4. Stretch the milk for 5-8 seconds, then sink the tip to create a whirlpool at 60-65 °C.
5. Wipe the wand with a damp cloth and purge again immediately after use.

## 6. Cleaning & descaling

**Daily:** empty and rinse the drip tray, knock out the basket, wipe the steam wand.

**Weekly:** remove the shower screen and rinse under hot water.

**Descaling (every 3 months):**

1. Dissolve one AUR-DESCALE sachet in 1 L warm water and pour into the tank.
2. Press and hold both brew buttons for 5 seconds until the ring blinks green.
3. Run half the solution through the group head, half through the steam wand.
4. Flush the tank twice with clean water and run two full brew cycles to rinse.

## 7. Error codes

- `E1` — No water detected. Refill the tank and press any button to resume.
- `E2` — Pump overheat. Unplug for 20 minutes, refill, and retry.
- `E3` — Thermoblock fault. Contact support; do not open the machine.
- `E4` — Descaling overdue. Run the descaling cycle in section 6 to clear.
